\begin{block}{The Question}
KV caches grow linearly with context. Two online fixes need no calibration:
\vspace{0.2cm}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Eviction} (StreamingLLM): keep sinks + recent tokens exactly, drop the rest.
  \item \textbf{Low-rank}: keep \emph{every} token, projected onto a rank-$R$ basis.
\end{itemize}
\vspace{0.2cm}
We tested \textbf{TKV}, a low-rank cache whose basis is fit on a 512-token
warmup and then \textbf{frozen} ($\mathcal{O}(Rd)$ per token), against a
corrected OjaKV and against eviction \textbf{at matched KV memory}.
\takeaway{When is keeping a compressed copy of every token\\ worth the memory?}
\end{block}

\begin{block}{Protocol}
\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}[x=1cm,y=1cm]
  \fill[navy!85] (0,0) rectangle (2.5,2.2);
  \fill[accent!70] (2.5,0) rectangle (16.5,2.2);
  \fill[good!75] (16.5,0) rectangle (23,2.2);
  \node[white,font=\small\bfseries] at (1.25,1.1) {sinks};
  \node[white,font=\small\bfseries] at (9.5,1.1) {middle: low-rank \emph{or} evicted};
  \node[white,font=\small\bfseries] at (19.75,1.1) {recent window};
  \node[font=\small,color=muted] at (1.25,-0.7) {exact};
  \node[font=\small,color=muted] at (19.75,-0.7) {exact};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.2cm}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Same layout for all:} 64 sinks + 256-token window. StreamingLLM gets the low-rank bytes as \emph{extra window}: $W' = W + TR/d + R$.
  \item \textbf{Models:} Mistral-7B, Qwen2.5-14B ($d{=}128$, 8 KV heads).
  \item \textbf{Data} (ctx 4096, 30 windows, same scored tokens for every method): WikiText-2, PG-19, and a \textbf{shift} set (512 prose tokens, then code).
  \item \textbf{Checks:} at $R{=}d$ or full window every hook reproduces exact logits.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\begin{block}{Finding 2: A Correct OjaKV Ties TKV}
A common streaming Oja's-rule implementation stores each token's
coefficients in the basis \emph{at arrival}, then reconstructs with the
\emph{final} basis, and its unnormalized step makes the basis snap to each new
token. The frames don't match, and on real keys the error came out
\textbf{above 1.0}: worse than storing zeros.
\vspace{0.3cm}
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{llccc}
\toprule
 & & TKV & OjaKV & StreamingLLM \\
\midrule
Mistral-7B & WikiText-2 & +5.6\% & +5.2\% & \textbf{\color{teal}+1.0\%} \\
           & shift      & +4.6\% & +4.2\% & \textbf{\color{teal}+0.2\%} \\[0.15cm]
Qwen2.5-14B & WikiText-2 & +5.3\% & +5.1\% & \textbf{\color{teal}+1.0\%} \\
           & shift      & +5.5\% & +4.9\% & \textbf{\color{teal}+0.2\%} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\\[0.2cm]
{\footnotesize\color{muted} PPL increase over exact, rank 64, 0.59$\times$ memory, same warmup for TKV and OjaKV.}
\end{center}
\vspace{0.2cm}
Fixed (normalized step, coefficients rotated into each new basis, same warmup
as TKV), OjaKV is within \textbf{0.5\%} of TKV, even under the prose$\to$code
shift. TKV's edge is \textbf{cost}, not quality.
\end{block}

\begin{block}{Takeaways \& Limits}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Default to \textbf{eviction} for language modeling; use low-rank only when evicted content must stay retrievable, at high rank, and check your model.
  \item Fit bases \textbf{pre-RoPE}; compare against \textbf{fixed} baselines at \textbf{matched memory}.
  \item Limits: 2K--8K context; 30 windows / 15 needle prompts per cell; exact-SVD warmup (best case); no quantization or H2O/SnapKV baseline.
\end{itemize}
\end{block}
